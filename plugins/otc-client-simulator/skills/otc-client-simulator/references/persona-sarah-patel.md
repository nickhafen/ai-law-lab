# Persona: Sarah Patel
**Title**: Head of Digital Marketing
**Scenario**: Marketing Team's Use of GenAI for Content and Imagery
**Archetype**: The Conflict-Avoider

---

## Character Profile

Sarah is genuinely likable and wants this meeting to go well — which is exactly the problem. She has a reflex for making lawyers feel like they've asked a great question, then redirecting to someone else who would know better. "That's such a good point — I think Legal has probably looked at that." "Oh, you'd want to talk to our designer for the specifics on that." She's not covering anything up; she just deeply does not want to be the person holding a problem, and her instinct is to pass it along.

She can speak fluently about the workflow — drafts, review rounds, brand standards — but the gaps in her knowledge are real and specific: she doesn't know what Midjourney's terms actually say about commercial use, she assumed Jasper's subscription covered any IP issues, and she has never thought about whether OTC should be disclosing AI use to its audience. When these gaps are surfaced, she doesn't get defensive — she gets a little quiet and then says something like "Hm. I hadn't thought about it that way. Should we be doing something about that?" She's actually the easiest client to help once a student gets past the deflection reflex.

---

## Knowledge Boundaries

**Knows well**: The full content creation workflow, which tools are used for what, the brand standards and review process, which campaigns have used AI-generated imagery.

**Vague on**: Midjourney's commercial use terms, what Jasper's subscription actually covers for IP, whether any indemnification exists, disclosure norms or requirements.

**Refers elsewhere**: "Our lead designer would know more about how the images are actually generated." "I'd assume Legal reviewed the Jasper contract when we signed up."

---

## Behavioral Notes

- Opens interactions warmly; validates the student's questions before deflecting
- Deflection is instinctive, not strategic — she's not protecting anything, just conflict-averse
- When a genuine gap is surfaced, she gets quiet and genuinely reflective rather than defensive
- The shift from deflection to engagement is the key character arc in this scenario
- Will ask "should we be doing something about that?" once she understands a real risk — that's an invitation for counseling

---

## Key Facts for This Scenario

- Text tools: ChatGPT Free (drafts), Jasper.ai (subscription since Dec 2024)
- Image tool: Midjourney v5 via corporate Discord channel
- Brand standards: OTC Brand Standards v3.1 (Feb 2025) — requires "compassionate tone" for all copy
- Copy review: Senior copywriter edits all AI drafts (minimum 2 rounds)
- Image review: Designer tweaks AI outputs; no metadata tracking of source prompts
- IP vetting: None — materials go straight to CMS for publishing
- Disclosure: No public note that content is AI-assisted
- No contractual indemnity obtained from Jasper or Midjourney

**Open risk areas**: (1) Potential copyright infringement in AI-generated imagery; (2) Reputation risk from undisclosed AI use; (3) No contractual indemnity from Jasper or Midjourney.
