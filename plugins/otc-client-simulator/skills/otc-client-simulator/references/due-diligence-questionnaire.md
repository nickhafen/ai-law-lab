# AI Risk and Ethics Due Diligence Questionnaire

---

## 1. Core Technology and System Overview

1. What type of AI model does your system use, and what is it designed to do?

2. What is the intended purpose and scope of the system's use — and are there any uses it was explicitly not designed for?

3. How would you explain how the system makes decisions to someone without a technical background?

4. Has the AI model replaced any previous processes or systems? If so, what improvements were sought and how have they been measured?

---

## 2. Data and Privacy Practices

### Data Collection and Usage

5. What types of data does the system collect or process (e.g., personal, sensitive, health-related, behavioral)?

6. What is the legal basis for collecting and using that data (e.g., consent, legitimate interest, contractual necessity)?

7. How is user consent obtained, documented, and managed — and what happens if a user withdraws consent?

8. Are inputs provided to the AI system used to further train or fine-tune the model, either by your company or by a third-party vendor?

9. Who owns the data collected by the AI system, and who owns the outputs it generates?

### Data Security

10. What measures are in place to protect user data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure?

11. Does the company have a documented incident response plan for data breaches or AI-related failures? Has it ever been tested or invoked?

12. Are regular security audits, penetration tests, or vulnerability assessments conducted — including any testing specific to the AI system itself?

### Bias and Fairness

13. How did you assess whether your training data was representative of your intended user base?

14. What specific steps were taken to identify and mitigate bias in the data and the model's outputs?

15. How are fairness metrics defined, measured, and monitored on an ongoing basis after deployment?

### Transparency and User Rights

16. What rights do users have with respect to their data — including access, correction, deletion, and the right to opt out of automated decision-making?

17. What is the company's data retention and deletion policy, and how is it enforced in practice?

18. How does the company communicate to users that an AI system is involved in decisions that affect them?

---

## 3. Model Development and Performance Testing

19. What is the source of the training data used for the AI model, and how was it licensed or obtained?

20. How was the model tested and validated before deployment — including any efficacy, bias, or adversarial safety testing?

21. What known limitations or failure modes does the system have, and how are those disclosed to users or mitigated operationally?

22. Has the system been tested specifically for performance differences across demographic groups (e.g., race, gender, age, disability status)?

---

## 4. Intellectual Property

23. Who owns the outputs generated by the AI system — the company, the user, the vendor, or is it unclear?

24. If the system was built using or integrated with third-party AI tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Midjourney, a commercial API), what do those tools' terms say about IP ownership of generated outputs?

25. What steps, if any, have been taken to vet AI-generated content or code for potential third-party IP infringement before use or publication?

26. Does the company disclose to its customers or the public when content has been generated with AI assistance?

---

## 5. Risk Management and Governance

27. Who within the company is responsible for overseeing AI ethics and risk management?

28. Is there a formal AI ethics committee or governance body? What is its composition, authority, and how often does it meet?

29. What AI-specific policies or governance frameworks has the company adopted, and how have those policies been operationalized into actual day-to-day practices?

30. How is human oversight built into the AI system's development, deployment, and ongoing monitoring?

31. Who is accountable when the AI system causes harm or makes a consequential error?

32. How does the company monitor deployed AI systems for errors, drift, misuse, or adverse impacts over time?

---

## 6. Vendor and Third-Party Management

33. If the AI system was developed or is operated by a third-party vendor, what due diligence was conducted before procurement?

34. What contractual protections exist with AI vendors — including audit rights, indemnification, data ownership, and breach notification obligations?

35. How does the company verify vendor claims about system performance, bias testing, or compliance — such as independently reviewing audit reports or underlying methodology?

36. What happens contractually if the vendor's system causes harm, discriminates unlawfully, or fails to meet performance benchmarks?

---

## 7. Regulatory Compliance

37. What laws and regulations apply to this AI system (e.g., HIPAA, EEOC requirements, state AI legislation, sector-specific rules), and how has the company assessed its compliance with each?

38. Has the company evaluated whether any state-specific AI laws apply to its operations — such as requirements for automated employment decision tools, algorithmic transparency, or AI disclosure obligations?

39. Is the company's AI use subject to any pending regulatory review, investigation, or litigation?

40. How does the company stay current with changes in applicable law, and who is responsible for translating legal developments into operational updates?

---

## 8. Ethical and Societal Considerations

41. Has the company conducted a formal assessment of the AI system's potential societal or disparate impacts on affected communities?

42. How does the system's development and deployment align with the company's stated values and ethical principles?

43. How does the company engage with affected stakeholders — including users, advocacy groups, and regulators — on AI ethics questions?

44. What safeguards are in place to prevent misuse of the system, including generation of harmful content, manipulation, or discriminatory outcomes?

45. Are there any past controversies, incidents, complaints, or regulatory inquiries involving the company's AI activities?

---

## 9. Documentation and Transparency

46. What documentation exists covering the technical and ethical aspects of the AI system's lifecycle — from development through deployment and monitoring?

47. Are there clear procedures for ongoing system monitoring, updating, and regulatory compliance review?

48. Does the company have a publicly available AI ethics policy or statement of principles?
